the gap today

Three problems that are usually handled by three different, disconnected systems.

Citizens today juggle separate portals for civic complaints, safety helplines, and legal information — each with its own process, and none of them talking to each other.

01 — grievances

Complaints go unresolved

Civic issues like broken infrastructure, sanitation or negligence are reported through slow, manual channels with little visibility into what happens after filing.

02 — safety

Reporting incidents is hard

Many hesitate to report unsafe situations because reporting tools are fragmented, unclear, or don't offer real-time support when it matters most.

03 — legal literacy

Rights stay out of reach

Legal language and unclear procedures keep people from understanding their rights or the correct steps to seek justice.

what's inside

Three modules, built to work as one shield.

Each module solves one problem on its own — together, they close the loop between reporting, responding, and understanding your rights.

Smart Public Grievance Portal

Citizens submit civic complaints online, attach evidence, and track the exact status of their case in real time — with automatic escalation if it sits unresolved past a set deadline.

  • Structured complaint form with category, location & priority
  • Live status tracking — pending, in review, resolved
  • Auto-escalation for cases past their deadline

Women Safety Module

A one-touch SOS sends live location to emergency contacts, alongside safe-route suggestions and secure incident reporting.

AI Legal Assistant

Describe a problem in plain language and get informational guidance on relevant rights, laws, and how to file a complaint.

the grievance flow

From report to resolution, in four steps.

01

Report

File a complaint with category, description, location and supporting evidence in under a minute.

02

Route

The complaint is logged with a unique ID and routed for review, visible to the citizen at every step.

03

Track

Status updates — pending, in review, resolved — are visible on the dashboard in real time.

04

Escalate

Cases left unresolved past a set window are automatically flagged for escalation.

System Architecture & Security

• Backend Gateway: http://localhost:8082
• Admin Token Protocol: HMAC-SHA256 Token (2-hour lifecycle)
• Citizen/Admin Isolation: Dual interceptors (CitizenAuthInterceptor & AdminAuthInterceptor)
• Role Authorization: Role verified on each protected REST endpoint
• Telemetry: Real database records without mock data
